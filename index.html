About My Work

Between sound, perception and musical experience.

My journey has moved across computer science, music performance, music education and, increasingly, the scientific study of musical perception.

I am particularly interested in understanding how we perceive continuous and fine-grained changes in pitch, how musical training influences auditory perception, and how culturally specific musical systems interact with more general mechanisms of hearing.

My background in Hindustani classical music and several years of music education also shape the questions I ask about musical learning, listening and embodied musical experience.

Research Interests

What I am interested in

My research interests sit at the intersection of auditory science, music cognition, musical culture and technology.

01

Pitch & Microtonality

Fine-pitch discrimination, continuous pitch, microtonal perception and the relationship between acoustic frequency and musical pitch.

02

Auditory & Music Perception

How listeners perceive musical sound, including questions involving pitch relationships, tuning, consonance, auditory discrimination and perceptual organisation.

03

Musical Training & Culture

How long-term musical experience, training and cultural environments may shape the way listeners hear and organise musical sound.

04

Music, Technology & Computation

Using digital audio, computational tools and experimental methods to study musical sound while connecting technological approaches with questions from perception and cognition.
